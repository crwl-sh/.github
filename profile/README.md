# crwl

See your site the way crawlers read it.

crwl scores how well AI agents and answer engines can find, read, and call any
website, then shows the exact fixes to climb. Most sites are built for people.
crwl shows you how the machines see them, and what to change.

Scan any site free at **[crwl.sh](https://crwl.sh)**, or from your terminal:

```sh
curl -fsSL crwl.sh/install | sh
crwl scan yoursite.com
```

The scanner is open source: **[crwl-sh/crwl-cli](https://github.com/crwl-sh/crwl-cli)**.
